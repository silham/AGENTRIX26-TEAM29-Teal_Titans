title: Passport e-Services and Appointments — Department of Immigration & Emigration

The Department of Immigration & Emigration provides an online e-Services portal for
passport applications. Through the portal an applicant can complete the application form,
make an online payment, and book an appointment for submission at the Head Office in
Battaramulla or a Regional Office.

Booking an appointment online reduces waiting time on the day of submission. The one-day
(same-day) service is available only at the Head Office and carries a higher fee. After
submission, applicants can check the status of their passport through the portal. A valid
National Identity Card and a certified application form are still required even when
applying online; the portal does not waive the standard document requirements.
