title: Medical Fitness Certificate for Driving — National Transport Medical Institute

The National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI) issues the medical certificate of fitness
to drive that is required when applying for or renewing a driving licence in Sri Lanka.

The medical examination checks vision and general fitness to operate a vehicle. Bring your
National Identity Card and your driving licence (or learner's details for a first licence)
to the examination. Pay the applicable examination fee. On passing, you receive the medical
certificate, which you then submit with your driving licence application or renewal at the
Department of Motor Traffic. The certificate has a limited validity period, so obtain it
close to the time you intend to submit your licence application.
