title: Driving Licence Renewal — Department of Motor Traffic

A driving licence in Sri Lanka must be renewed before it expires to continue driving
legally. Renewal is handled by the Department of Motor Traffic (RMV) and authorised
centres.

To renew your driving licence you need:
- A valid National Identity Card for identity verification.
- Your current (expiring) driving licence.
- A medical certificate of fitness to drive, obtained from the National Transport Medical
  Institute (NTMI) or an authorised medical officer.
- A completed renewal application form.
- Payment of the renewal fee.

First check the expiry date on your licence so you renew within the permitted window.
Obtain the medical fitness certificate, then submit the application together with your NIC
and current licence at the Department of Motor Traffic or an authorised centre, and pay
the fee. You can then track the renewal and collect the renewed licence. If your NIC is
lost, obtain a duplicate NIC first, as a valid NIC is required for the renewal.
