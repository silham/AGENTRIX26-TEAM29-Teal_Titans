title: Applying for a National Identity Card (First Time) — Department for Registration of Persons

Every Sri Lankan citizen who has reached the eligible age is entitled to a National
Identity Card (NIC) issued by the Department for Registration of Persons (DRP). The NIC is
the primary proof of identity used across government services.

For a first-time NIC you typically need your birth certificate, the application form
(certified by the Grama Niladhari of your division), and the required photographs. School
or other supporting documents may be requested to confirm identity for younger applicants.
Submit the certified application at your Divisional Secretariat. Because the NIC is required
for passports, driving licences, banking, and many other services, keeping it valid and
replacing it promptly if lost is important.
