title: Replacing a Lost or Damaged National Identity Card — Department for Registration of Persons

If your National Identity Card is lost, stolen or damaged, you can apply for a duplicate
NIC from the Department for Registration of Persons (DRP). You should already have been
issued an NIC number; the duplicate re-issues a card against your existing record.

Documents required for a duplicate NIC:
- A police report for the lost or stolen card. Report the loss at your nearest police
  station and obtain the complaint reference. This police report is mandatory for lost
  card replacements.
- The original birth certificate.
- A duplicate NIC application (Form RG-D-01), completed and certified by the Grama
  Niladhari of your division.
- Photographs meeting the DRP specification.
- Payment of the applicable fee.

Submit the certified application together with the supporting documents at your
Divisional Secretariat or the DRP, and pay the fee. Collect the duplicate National
Identity Card once it is issued. A valid NIC is required for many downstream services such
as passport applications and driving licence renewals, so recovering it first unblocks
those procedures.
