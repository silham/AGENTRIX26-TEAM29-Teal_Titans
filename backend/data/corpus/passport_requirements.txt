title: How to Apply for a Sri Lankan Passport — Department of Immigration & Emigration

To apply for a Sri Lankan passport you must be a Sri Lankan citizen and hold a valid
National Identity Card (NIC). A valid NIC is mandatory; if your NIC is lost, stolen or
damaged you must first obtain a duplicate NIC from the Department for Registration of
Persons before a passport can be issued.

Required documents for a passport application:
- A valid National Identity Card.
- The original birth certificate.
- Two recent colour photographs taken against a white background, sized 35mm x 45mm,
  meeting the published photo specification.
- A completed application Form K-35-A, certified by an authorised officer such as the
  Grama Niladhari of your division.
- Payment of the prescribed application fee (normal service or one-day service).

Applications may be submitted in person at the Head Office in Battaramulla, at a Regional
Office, or through the online appointment system at the e-Services portal. Applicants for
the one-day service must attend the Head Office. After submission you can track the
issuance status and collect the passport once processing is complete.
