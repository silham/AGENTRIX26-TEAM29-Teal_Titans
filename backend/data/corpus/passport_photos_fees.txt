title: Passport Photo Specification and Fees — Department of Immigration & Emigration

Passport photographs must be recent colour photographs taken against a plain white
background. The face must be clearly visible, with a neutral expression and no head
covering except for religious reasons. Photographs that are blurred, over-exposed, taken
against a coloured background, or that crop the head incorrectly will be rejected.

Fees depend on the service type. The normal service has a lower fee with standard
processing time, while the one-day (same-day) service carries a higher fee and is handled
only at the Head Office in Battaramulla. Fees can be paid at the designated bank counter
or through the online payment option on the e-Services portal. Keep the payment receipt;
it must be presented with the application.
