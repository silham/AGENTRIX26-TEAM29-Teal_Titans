title: Grama Niladhari Certification of Documents — Government of Sri Lanka

Many government applications require certification by the Grama Niladhari (GN) of your
division. The Grama Niladhari is the local government officer who verifies a citizen's
identity and residence and certifies application forms.

Both the duplicate National Identity Card application and the passport application form
must be certified by the Grama Niladhari before submission. To get a form certified, visit
the Grama Niladhari for the division where you reside with the completed form, your
National Identity Card (or supporting identity documents if the NIC is lost), and any
required photographs. The GN confirms your details and signs and stamps the form. This
certification step is a common prerequisite, so complete it before travelling to the
issuing department.
