title: Obtaining a Copy of a Birth Certificate — Registrar General's Department

A certified copy of a birth certificate is issued by the Registrar General's Department
(RGD) and the Divisional Secretariat of the area where the birth was registered.

To obtain a copy you generally need to provide the district and Divisional Secretariat of
birth, the date of birth, the full name as registered, and, if known, the existing
certificate or entry number. The applicant's relationship to the person named on the
certificate may be requested. Submit the request at the relevant Divisional Secretariat or
through the RGD online service and pay the prescribed fee. Birth certificates are commonly
required as supporting documents for passport applications, duplicate NIC applications,
school admissions, and marriage registration.
